Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date, along with an itemised
receipt. Reports submitted after this window may be declined at the discretion of finance.

Meals while travelling on company business are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Alcohol is only
reimbursable when entertaining a client, and must be itemised separately on the expense claim.

Reimbursement is paid out with the next payroll cycle after the report is approved by your manager
and finance. Most approvals are completed within five business days of submission.
