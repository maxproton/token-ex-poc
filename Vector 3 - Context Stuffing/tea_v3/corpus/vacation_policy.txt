New full-time employees accrue 15 vacation days per calendar year, credited at the start of each
month. Vacation requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance
for approval by your manager.

Unused vacation days may be carried over into the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 5 days.
Any balance above that cap is forfeited on December 31st unless local law requires otherwise.

Employees who have completed five years of service accrue an additional 3 vacation days per year,
for a total of 18 days. This increase takes effect automatically on your service anniversary date.
