The standard plan includes up to 10 team members and 50GB of storage. Additional seats can be
added at any time from the billing page and are prorated for the current billing cycle.

Data exports are available in CSV or JSON format from the account settings page. Exports of more
than 1 million rows are processed as a background job and emailed to you when ready.

Support tickets submitted through the in-app widget are typically answered within one business day.
Enterprise customers have access to a dedicated Slack channel for faster response times.
